We make advanced materials from commonly available ingredients

Impossible Fibers creates advanced materials, using manufacturing methods we learn from how biology makes fibers. As a frontier research contractor (FRC), we are advancing what's possible to create with protein fibers, from sensors that give robots a sense of touch to hollow-core optical fibers for telecommunications. FRCs are a model championed by ARIA and Renaissance Philanthropy: technically ambitious organizations that build working technology for real users, funded through research contracts and grants.
