What we commit to

  • Release earlyDatasets, results and tools as they mature, not held for one final paper.
  • Publish more than papersProtocols, open hardware, informative failures and open problems.
  • Ask for critiqueWe invite people to use our tools and data, and to test their rigor.
  • Show how it works in a companyWe document where the line between open and protected falls, so others have a worked example.

Reproduce our work, or build shared datasets with us

We're looking for characterization partners and labs willing to reproduce our methods and discuss the results in public.
